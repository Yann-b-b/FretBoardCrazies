## Common questions

### The drill does not react when I play

Check the tuner screen first — if it shows a moving frequency, the microphone is working and the
problem is in the drill; if it shows nothing, it is the microphone.

Things that stop note detection:

- **Permission denied.** Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › FretBoard Crazies.
- **Too quiet.** Pitch detection ignores signal below an amplitude threshold. Play closer to the
  device, or louder.
- **More than one string ringing.** Detection is monophonic. Mute the strings you are not playing.
- **A chord.** Same reason. The drill wants one clear note at a time.

### Can I use the app without an instrument?

Yes. Choose **"I'll tap the fretboard"** on the welcome screen, or turn on **Touch mode** in
Settings › Game. Drills are answered by tapping the fret instead of playing it.

### It hears the wrong note, an octave off

Low notes on a bass, and the lowest strings of a guitar, have weak fundamentals that pitch trackers
sometimes read an octave high. Playing nearer the bridge and letting the note settle usually fixes
it.

### Why is it landscape only?

A fretboard is a wide object. In portrait it would either be too small to tap accurately or cropped
so that the frets you need are off screen.

### Where is my progress stored?

On your device. Nothing is uploaded — the app makes no network connections. Deleting the app deletes
your progress, and there is currently no way to transfer it to another device.

### Which instruments are supported?

Guitar and bass, switchable in Settings › Instrument.

## Privacy

See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md). Short version: nothing is collected, nothing is
transmitted, and microphone audio is analysed on-device and never recorded.
