# Changelog

## v1.1.0 — 2026-08-11
### Highlights
- First App Store release: fretboard drill, tuner, progress and settings
- Play the prompted note on your instrument and the app hears you
- No instrument to hand? Choose "I'll tap the fretboard" and answer by tapping
- Practise guitar or bass, pick your strings, and limit the fret range while learning
- Track accuracy and reaction time per note, build combos, and climb the belt ranks

## v1.0.0 — 2026-07-05
### Highlights
- Multi-instrument support: guitar and bass, switchable in Settings
- Per-instrument string presets, progress, and belt ranks
- Combo escalation with flame tiers, animations, and tiered sound cues
- Welcome screen and adaptive iOS/macOS layout
- Note-name tuner
