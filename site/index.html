FretBoard Crazies

Learn every note on the fretboard. The app listens to your instrument and tells you whether you found the right note — or you can tap the fretboard if you're practising away from your guitar.

What it does

  • Drill — the app names a note and a string; you play it, and it hears you.
  • Progress — accuracy and reaction time per note, combo streaks, and belt ranks from white to black.
  • Tuner — live note name, frequency, and signal readout.
  • Settings — pick your strings, limit the fret range, switch between guitar and bass.

Privacy in one line

No account, no ads, no analytics, and no network connections at all. Microphone audio is analysed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Full detail on the privacy page.
